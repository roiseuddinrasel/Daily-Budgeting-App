Bills & recurring payments

Track what's due and what's paid

How bills relate to transactions. A bill is a reminder, not money spent. When you click Pay, DailyBudget can record one linked expense for that due date and then moves a recurring bill to its next due date. Skip advances the date without recording anything. Advancing never creates an expense on its own, and the database rejects a second payment for the same bill period.

Savings goals

Plan for what matters

Goals are plans, not accounts. "Saved" amounts are numbers you enter; they are not verified by any bank. A goal marked earmarked in balance is money already inside your tracked balance that you've set aside — it is subtracted once to get "Available to spend". A goal held elsewhere (e.g. a separate savings account you don't track here) is shown for progress only and never added to your balance.

Privacy & security

  • No account, no server. Everything you enter is stored in this browser's local storage on this device. Nothing is uploaded — the page is not allowed to make network requests.
  • Not synced. Other devices and browsers won't see your data. Use Backup & restore to move it.
  • Clearing your browser data deletes it, and private/incognito windows forget it when closed. Keep a backup.
  • Anyone using this browser profile can open it. Don't use DailyBudget on a shared or public computer for real figures.
  • Never enter bank passwords, full card numbers or account numbers. Amounts are what you type — not verified by any bank.
  • This website's code is public on GitHub Pages; your data is not part of it.
