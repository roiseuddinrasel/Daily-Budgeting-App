Private by design

Your financial records are protected by the database itself, not just by what the screen shows.

  • Row Level Security. Every record is tied to your account and the database refuses to return it to anyone else.
  • No bank credentials. DailyBudget never asks for bank passwords, card numbers or account numbers. Please don't type them into notes.
  • No tracking, no AI. No analytics scripts, and your transactions are never sent to third-party AI services.
  • Your data, your call. Export everything as CSV or JSON, or permanently delete your account and all records.
  • Open source front end. This site's code is public on GitHub Pages; your data is not. Amounts are what you enter — they are not verified by any bank.

Bills & recurring payments

Track what's due and what's paid

How bills relate to transactions. A bill is a reminder, not money spent. When you click Pay, DailyBudget can record one linked expense for that due date and then moves a recurring bill to its next due date. Skip advances the date without recording anything. Advancing never creates an expense on its own, and the database rejects a second payment for the same bill period.

Savings goals

Plan for what matters

Goals are plans, not accounts. "Saved" amounts are numbers you enter; they are not verified by any bank. A goal marked earmarked in balance is money already inside your tracked balance that you've set aside — it is subtracted once to get "Available to spend". A goal held elsewhere (e.g. a separate savings account you don't track here) is shown for progress only and never added to your balance.

Privacy & security

  • Your records are protected by Row Level Security in the database: requests for another user's data return nothing, regardless of what this page does.
  • This website's source code is public (GitHub Pages). It contains only a public "publishable" key that cannot bypass those protections.
  • Your session is kept by the Supabase client in this browser's storage. Log out on shared computers.
  • Never enter bank passwords, full card numbers or account numbers in descriptions or notes.
  • Nothing you enter is sent to analytics or AI services.
